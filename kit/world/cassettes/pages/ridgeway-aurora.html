Ridgeway Builders wins Aurora logistics warehouse contract

BuildTrade News staff, 21 October 2026

Chicago general contractor Ridgeway Builders has been selected to build the Aurora logistics warehouse, a $48M warehouse project for a regional distribution company.

Groundbreaking is planned for April 2027, subject to final permits. The building will include a mezzanine office block, cold-storage bays and heavy racking throughout.

Ridgeway said it would announce its major subcontractors and suppliers early next year.
