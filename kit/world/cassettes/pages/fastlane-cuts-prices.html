Fastlane Supply cuts fastener prices 8% for Q4

BuildTrade News staff, 14 October 2026

Fastlane Supply is cutting prices 8% on fasteners and anchors for contractor accounts, effective 15 October to 31 December. The distributor said the cut is meant to win share from regional suppliers ahead of the 2027 contract season.

The price cut excludes power-tool accessories, which stay at current list prices. Fastlane did not say whether the lower prices will continue into next year.

Analysts expect other distributors to respond selectively, matching prices only on high-volume accounts rather than across their whole catalogs.
