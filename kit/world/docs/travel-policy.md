# Larkspur Supply travel policy

Applies to every employee travelling on company business. Questions and exceptions go to the Travel Desk (travel@larkspur.example) before you book.

## Flights

- Economy for flights under 6 hours; business class not allowed; basic economy allowed.
- Book at least 7 days before departure, otherwise manager approval is needed.

## Hotels

- Hotels: Chicago cap $260 a night including taxes; use the conference block when it's within the cap.
- Other cities follow the Travel Desk's city rate list.

## Meals

- Customer dinners: up to $90 a person including tip.
- Keep itemized receipts and list every guest on the expense report.

## Approvals

- Trips over $1,500 in total need CRO approval.
- Record any approval (who approved and when) on the booking.
